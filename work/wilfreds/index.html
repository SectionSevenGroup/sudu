Hospitality

Wilfreds

02 / 19
Location
Edmonton, AB
Scope
Interiors, Material Direction
Status
Completed

A standalone brick heritage building, rebuilt from the inside out as a single hospitality idea.

Custom millwork, soft colour, natural materials, and playful mural work give the room a warm, residential scale. Space and millwork by the studio, mural by Vanguard Works, brand development with Shaun Brandt: a demonstration of how closely identity and physical place can be built together.
