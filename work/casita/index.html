Private Residential

Casita

04 / 19
Location
Costa Rica
Scope
Architecture, Interiors
Status
In development

A single family residence lifted into the palm canopy of a hillside site.

A compact volume raised on concrete pilotis, opening the main living level to the treetops and long views while the ground plane stays planted and shaded beneath. Deep overhangs, screened openings, and a pared palette of white render, warm timber, and glass are shaped around the tropical climate.
