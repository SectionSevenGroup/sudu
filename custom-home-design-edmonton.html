Services — Residential

Custom Home Design in Edmonton

A custom home is the most personal project in architecture. SuDu Studio designs new residences across Edmonton, Alberta and Western Canada, carrying one disciplined process from the first site walk to the day you move in.

01

How we design a home

Every residence starts with the site and the way you actually live: light, grade, views, privacy, and the rhythms of a household. We test massing options early so decisions are made with your eyes open, then resolve materials, interiors, and detail as one continuous idea rather than a shell with finishes applied later. Our studio holds architecture and interior design under one roof, so the building and its rooms are never designed by different hands.

02

What the work includes

Concept and feasibility studies, schematic design, development permits, construction documentation, and support through construction. We work with trusted builders across Alberta and can help you select one early, when their pricing insight matters most. For regulated services, work is carried by the studio’s registered professionals.

03

Recent residential work

Current and recent projects include Westshore Estates, a custom hillside residence in the Okanagan developed from massing studies through exterior material resolution, and a private residence and gallery in West Vancouver organized around a personal art collection.
