Services — Commercial

Commercial + Retail Design in Edmonton

Commercial space is a brand promise made physical. SuDu Studio designs workplaces, retail environments, and building repositioning across Alberta, from single tenancies to city-block renewals.

01

Repositioning and workplace

Our commercial work includes ATB Place Reimagined, a renewal of the concourse, podium, and exterior plazas at one of downtown Edmonton’s major intersections, developed in collaboration with DIALOG. We help owners see what an underperforming asset can become, then carry the design through approvals and construction.

02

Retail and banking environments

The studio led strategic planning, concept development, and design direction for ATB Financial’s Branches of the Future, a multi-year initiative reimagining flagship and neighbourhood locations across Alberta, and designed The Helm, a hybrid retail and hospitality environment in Edmonton.

03

For developers and landlords

Feasibility and concept studies, tenant-ready design guidelines, and design direction for allied practices. Early, decision-grade drawings that make pro formas honest.
